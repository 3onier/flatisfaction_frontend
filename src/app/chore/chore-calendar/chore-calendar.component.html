What is a schedule?

When creating a chore the schedule i.e. who is in charge each time isn't generated automatically.
after you saved you chores and select each responsible member then you get to generate a schedule. Each member gets told when to do each particular chore

Clear, Clear all and generate

Clear all will clear the schedule in the future.
Clear will clear the schedule within your selected timeframe. This way you can for example clear out when everyone is out the flat or on holiday
Generate will generate the timetable for your selected timeframe.

How will the Chores be distributed?

When creating a chore you can tell how much effort the chore is. A chore with effort of 1 is half a tidious as one with effort of 2. The algorithm will try to give each member of the flat equal number of effort point over the selected timeframe.

Example

When setting up the chore you decided that bringing out rubbish has 1 effort point and cleaning kitchen has 5 effort point. In other words cleaning the kitchen is 5 times more effort then bringing out the rubbish. The algorithm will give one member of the flat the task to clean the kitchen. Now each other flat member has to take out the rubbish 5 times before any other tasks gets assigned to you
